How to Use This Dashboard
DATA SOURCE TOGGLE
SF-Led — requires a matching Salesforce OM Opportunity. Shows the full Lead → Funded funnel including contact rates and lead type breakdowns. Use this for pipeline quality and LO engagement analysis.

Byte-Led — every Byte file created regardless of whether an SF lead exists. Starts at App Complete and shows App Complete → Funded only. No contact rate (SF data unavailable for unmatched files). Use this for production volume and true funded counts — it captures ~87 more funded loans than the SF-led view because some loans close without ever being linked to an SF opportunity.
COHORT YEAR
Filters by the year the lead was created in Salesforce (SF-Led) or the Byte file was created (Byte-Led). Use 2025 for fully seasoned data with reliable funded counts. Use 2026 to see current pipeline activity — funded counts will be understated since most 2026 leads have not had enough time to complete the funnel.
LOAN OFFICER FILTER
Filter to an individual LO or view the full team. Other LO captures all leads and files assigned to LOs outside the current team of four — including former LOs, system default users, and leads assigned to current LOs before their start date (pre-join reassignments). When viewing All LOs, Other LO data is included in aggregate totals and shown as a separate grayed row in the detail panel.
LEAD TYPE FILTER
Available in SF-Led mode only. Filters by the Brokerage Service Name or OM Lead Source field in Salesforce. Move First / Equity Advance is the dominant source at ~70% of all leads. Buy-only Lead Sale is a newer channel started in 2026 — insufficient data for meaningful conversion analysis yet.
FUNNEL STAGES
Lead — OM Opportunity created in Salesforce (SF-Led only)
App Started — Blend application initiated, based on SF stage or presence of a Byte file (SF-Led only)
App Complete — Byte file exists, meaning Blend app was fully submitted
TRID — Subject property address added to the application; loan is officially in-progress
Lock — Rate locked. Resolved loans only — files still actively in-progress are excluded from this count
Funded — Loan closed and funded. Bridge loans, second mortgages, and pre-2025 cohorts excluded
INTERACTING WITH THE FUNNEL
Click one stage to open a detail panel showing contact rate, per-LO counts, and lead type breakdown for that stage. Click two stages to see the conversion rate between any pair (e.g. App Complete → Funded). Click a selected stage again to deselect. In Byte-Led mode, Lead and App Started are grayed out and not selectable.
CONTACT RATE
Available in SF-Led mode only. A lead is considered contacted if there was an outbound call attempt or inbound answered call logged in Dialpad within the selected window (30 or 90 days). Voicemail attempts count. Does not capture SMS, email, or outreach made outside Dialpad. Contact rate is not available for Other LO leads since many are unmatched to SF records.
TRID → LOCK RATE
This dashboard shows ~87% TRID → Lock because bridge loans and second mortgages are excluded. The company-wide figure of ~46% includes bridge loans, which have Application Dates set but a 0% lock rate — they inflate the denominator without contributing to the numerator. The two numbers are not directly comparable.
LOCK → FUNDED RATE
Sourced directly from Byte (resolved loans only) regardless of which data source mode is active. SF-matched loans skew toward higher-converting files and are not used for this metric. Target is 75%.
DATA SEASONING
Funded counts take ~9 months to reach 96% of their final value. 2025 cohorts are fully seasoned. 2026 cohorts (especially Mar–May) will show understated funded numbers until they mature. Use Lock as the primary conversion benchmark for recent cohorts.

HOW TO EXPORT
↓ Full Guide (.docx)
1 Salesforce Open LO Capacity – Lead Conversion. Click the ▼ dropdown next to Edit → Export. Select Details Only and Excel (.xlsx), then Export.
2 Byte Find LO Capacity – In Progress Funded in Reports. Double-click to run — no filter prompts. File downloads automatically to your Byte reports folder.
3 Dialpad Go to Analytics → All Calls. Set date filter to one calendar year. Click Export dropdown → Export as CSV. When prompted select Call Logs, then Send Report. Download from the email you receive. Repeat for each year. You can select all files at once in the upload picker.
4 Upload Select all three files using the pickers above, then click Process & Upload. Processing runs in your browser (~5–15 sec). Data is shared instantly with anyone who has the URL.
